Slide Lab: what to do, step by step, and what to expect from Claude

Slide Lab is a set of add-ons for Claude (the AI assistant you chat with in the Claude desktop app) that builds a client-branded PowerPoint deck with you. You bring the message, the audience, your notes and the client's PowerPoint template; Claude helps shape the story, designs every slide on that template, lets you pick the designs, checks the result and hands you a normal, editable .pptx file.

Read section 1 for the whole picture in under 3 minutes. Use section 4 when you are in the middle of a deck and want to know what happens next, how long it takes and what to do if something goes wrong.

Accurate as of 2 Oct 2026 (Slide Lab version f818f20, the code's short ID). Timings measured on one 10-slide build, Oct 2026 (3 options per slide, session folder on the local drive, designs by Claude Opus 5.5). Rows marked Estimate were not timed on that build.
Newest copy: always in your Slide Lab folder, C:\Users\<you>\.claude\skills\Slide-Lab-Tutorial.html, updated when Slide Lab updates. A copy someone emailed you may be out of date.
The running example. Throughout this guide we follow one real Slide Lab build: a 10-slide deck called "Meridian: Southeast Asia entry". Meridian is a made-up company; its template and every number are invented. The timings in this guide were measured on that build. You can open its files, which sit in the same folder as this guide: the storyline, the review page (the real first-round page, 3 options per slide) and the finished deck.

10Common questions

Do I need to know how to code or design?
No. You talk to Claude in plain English and click buttons on two web pages. Claude asks before it runs anything on your computer; click Allow when it does.
What if I don't like any of the designs for a slide?
Click REPLACE THESE and say in the notes what you want different. You get 1 new design by default; say "with 3 options" if you want alternatives. For a small change, pick the closest option and add a quick-feedback button or a note instead.
Why do I paste a command back into Claude?
The review pages are files on your computer and cannot talk to Claude directly. The copied command carries your exact choices with a check code, so nothing is retyped or misread. Paste it unchanged.
Can I keep my work in OneDrive?
Keep the session folder on your computer's own drive (C:\Users\<you>\Slide Lab\sessions\). In OneDrive, builds are noticeably slower and previews can time out. Copy the finished deck to OneDrive or SharePoint afterwards. The template itself can live anywhere.
Can I edit the charts afterwards?
Charts are drawn from shapes for a clean, on-brand look, so they are not linked to a datasheet. Either ask Claude to rebuild the slide with new numbers, or say "Export the chart data to Excel" and paste the sheet into think-cell or a normal PowerPoint chart.
Is my client's template safe?
Yes. Slide Lab works on its own copy and never changes your original file. Your work stays on your computer.
Can it work on a deck I already have?
Yes, option 6 in the menu: fix text or numbers (6a), redesign a slide and drop it back into your deck (6b), or refresh a recurring deck with this cycle's text (6c). It always writes a new file.
Does it work on a Mac?
Yes. The install guide covers Windows and Mac. Paths look different on a Mac (your home folder instead of C:\Users\<you>).
