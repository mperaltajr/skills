## 2026-05-26 — F2: Visual disambiguation of two placeholder mechanisms

Pre-Workshop Scope Alignment feedback log identified slide-builder emits visually-identical bracketed placeholders for two semantically-different cases:

1. **Intentional presenter prompts** — `[add footnote here or delete]` / `[add source here or delete]` from `add_footer()` defaults. Cross-skill convention (F1, landed). slide-qc allowlists as Advisory.
2. **Honest Hardline #2 placeholders** — e.g., `[Kickoff activities — fill from 30-Day Sprint Master Plan]`. Worker honors "no fabrication" rule; brief didn't enumerate cells.

Both render as `[brackets]`. QC and presenter can't tell them apart at a glance.

**Proposed disambiguation:**
- Intentional prompts → italic gray text with leading `[?]` icon, e.g., `[?] add footnote or delete`
- Hardline #2 placeholders → dotted-border container with leading `[—]` icon, e.g., `[—] activity content — fill from <named source>`

**Implementation scope:** add visual styling to `add_footer()` (and any other helper that emits intentional prompts) + add a new helper for Hardline #2 placeholders that workers must use instead of bare bracketed text. Updates the QC vision-pass prompt to distinguish severities by visual style, not just text content.

Effort: MEDIUM (~2 hrs). Defer until v0.2 design pass.

## 2026-05-26 — F6: Hardline #2 placeholder recovery via named source

Pre-Workshop Scope Alignment feedback log: when the brief didn't enumerate slide-7 cell content, the worker correctly emitted Hardline #2 placeholders like `[Kickoff activities — fill from 30-Day Sprint Master Plan]`. The recovery path was manual: Mario dispatched a sub-agent to extract content from the named source PPTX, then hand-edited `slide_07/option_C.py` to replace the placeholders.

**Proposed improvement.** If the brief's per-slide block points at a named source artifact ("fill from <path>"), allow the worker to read that artifact and enumerate from it — bounded by an explicit budget (max N reads, max M sub-elements per slide) so the worker doesn't go on a fishing expedition.

**Risk.** This is a relaxation of Hardline #2 in a specific direction. Needs design careful enough that workers don't start fabricating "obvious" enumerations from titles alone. Source must be EXPLICITLY cited in the brief.

Effort: HIGH (v0.2+). Park until F2 lands first (visual disambiguation makes the audit trail of "what was placeholder vs what was filled from source" legible).
