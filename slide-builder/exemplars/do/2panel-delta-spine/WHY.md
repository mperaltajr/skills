# comparison / 2panel-delta-spine

**Family:** Comparison

**What this is.** Two equal-width columns (Option A / Option B) with a narrow BRAND_PRIMARY-filled center spine (100px wide) between them. The spine carries a BRAND_ACCENT chip in the center showing the key delta or differentiator. Column headers are dark-cap style. No convergence band at the bottom — the two options remain visually opposed.

**Use when.** True head-to-head comparison where you want the DELTA to be the visual hero (not the conclusion). The spine literally sits between the two sides and shows what separates them. Use this when the audience needs to feel the tension, not just be told which option wins.

**What makes the structure work.**
- Center spine as the mediator: the BRAND_PRIMARY spine visually separates A from B while the BRAND_ACCENT delta chip inside it marks the single most important differentiating factor.
- "VS" label at the top of the spine + "RECOMMEND" marker at the bottom: the spine tells the story from top (opposition) to bottom (resolution) without requiring a separate conclusion band.
- Column headers use the same dark-cap pattern (BRAND_PRIMARY top strip, WHITE text) — both options get equal visual weight, reinforcing the "fair comparison" impression.
- One accent moment: the BRAND_ACCENT delta chip. Nothing else is BRAND_ACCENT.
- Bold ceiling: title (1) + 2 column headings (2) = 3. Well under the 5-ceiling.

**Don't do this with this layout.**
- Don't shade one column darker than the other to "hint" at a winner — this breaks the fair-comparison impression the spine is designed to preserve. Use the spine's "RECOMMEND" text instead.
- Don't make the spine any narrower than 80px — it needs enough width to hold a readable chip label.
- Don't add a convergence band at the bottom — that's 2panel-convergence. This variant is deliberately end-open.

**Structural delta from 2panel-convergence.**
- 2panel-delta-spine: opposed columns, no unifying band. Delta chip on center spine = the key visual.
- 2panel-convergence: the two panels merge into a bottom BRAND_PRIMARY convergence band that delivers the shared conclusion.
- Clearly distinct at thumbnail: center dark strip vs. full-width bottom band.
