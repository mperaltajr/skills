# three-column / dark-header-cards

**Family:** Three-column parallel

**What this is.** Three equal-width cards spanning the full body. Each card has a BRAND_PRIMARY dark header cap (top 155px) with a white eyebrow and bold white heading inside, and a light CARD_BG body with 3 bullet rows and a bottom meta line. A single BRAND_ACCENT 6px left-edge strip on Card 1 marks it as primary.

**Use when.** MECE pillars, options, or themes where each item has a name, 3 supporting points, and a meta reference. Prefer this over icon-circles when the content is text-heavy and the "declaration" weight should come from the dark cap, not from a decorative icon.

**What makes the structure work.**
- The dark header cap at ~33% of card height creates a strong horizontal banding rhythm across all three cards — the eye reads all three headers as a group before moving to any body content.
- Card 1 BRAND_ACCENT strip signals "this is the primary pillar" while staying within the one-accent-moment rule (cards 2–3 use 1px CARD_BORDER).
- Bold ceiling: title (1) + 3 card headings (3) = 4. ≤5 ceiling honored. Eyebrows, bullet text, meta lines NOT bold.
- Bullets use 8px BRAND_PRIMARY square markers (not BRAND_ACCENT — the accent is the card-1 strip).

**Don't do this with this layout.**
- Don't use dark caps AND icon circles — that doubles the "emphasis zone" per card and competes with itself.
- Don't use BRAND_ACCENT for the header cap — the cap color is BRAND_PRIMARY (see 5b icon containers rule: BRAND_ACCENT burns on containers).
- Don't make all three cards equal visual weight when one pillar is the dominant one — use the accent strip to differentiate.

**Structural delta from 3pillar-icon-circles.**
- Dark-header-cards: the dark area is a full-width band across the top of each card. No circle icon containers.
- 3pillar-icon-circles: icon circles float above the card with the card body entirely light. The icon shape is the visual.
- These are clearly distinct at thumbnail size (banded dark top vs. floating circle).
