# single-finding-v2 (hero-metric variant)

**What this is.** ONE massive number dominates the slide. A short section label sits above it, a single supporting sentence sits below. No bullets, no cards, no chart — the number IS the slide.

**Distinct from v1.** The existing `single-finding` exemplar is a QUALITATIVE hero takeaway claim ("X is a thought partner, not a slide machine") with three parallel supporting bullets. This v2 is the QUANTITATIVE sibling — the skeleton's declared page_types include "hero-stat", "big-number", and "key-metric", which v1 does not serve. Different visual anchor (giant number vs. text claim), different supporting structure (one sentence vs. three bullets), different accent strategy (the number itself is the accent, no rule).

**What makes it strong.**
- **Hero metric at 180pt BRAND_ACCENT** is the entire slide. Sized so big it cannot be misread as anything but THE finding. Top-anchored in a tall box so the number sits high and the eye drops naturally to the supporting line.
- **One accent moment, executed as the metric itself.** No accent rule, no accent square. BRAND_ACCENT shows up exactly once — on the number. Section label and supporting line are TEXT_DARK; no gray (per the TEXT_MID/FAINT alias).
- **Section label uppercase + letter-spaced**, 14px, TEXT_DARK. Anchors the metric in a category without competing with it. Above the number, not below.
- **Supporting line at 18px TEXT_DARK, NOT bold.** Above the body floor; large enough to be the metric's lieutenant; small enough that the number wins. ~78% width — reads as a statement, not a paragraph.
- **Bold ceiling honored.** Title (1) + metric (1) = 2 bold text runs. Section label and supporting line are unbold.

**Reach for this when.** The slide is ONE quantitative finding — a percentage, a multiple, a rating, a dollar figure — and one sentence of "so what." Editorial emphasis is "this number reframes the conversation." If you have 2+ numbers or need to compare, use a different pattern.

**Patterns to copy.** Massive `font_size_pt=180` brand-accent metric as the sole accent moment; uppercase + letter-spaced section label as a small categorical anchor above the metric; ~78%-width supporting line below.
