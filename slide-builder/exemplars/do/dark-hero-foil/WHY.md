# dark-hero-foil

**What this is.** Asymmetric cover/divider split: a 35% left BRAND_PRIMARY block carries the dark hero title; the 65% right white panel carries supporting definitions and a meta block. The contrast IS the visual interest.

**What makes it strong.**
- **Side panel runs FULL canvas height** (0→720). Not 50→670 — the dark block goes edge-to-edge, eliminating any white margin on the left and creating the strongest possible asymmetric split.
- **Hero title 40px white bold, bottom-anchored** inside a tall box (y=180, h=180). Capped at 40 (not 48) because the narrower left block can't fit 48px multi-word titles cleanly.
- **Single 64px BRAND_ACCENT rule inside the dark block,** under the tagline, at y=460. That's the one accent moment — and it sits ON the dark ground where it pops hardest.
- **Right white panel uses TEXT_MID eyebrow + TEXT_DARK sub-heading + TEXT_MID body** — neutral palette only. Numerals (01/02/03) at TEXT_FAINT 14px non-bold, letter-spaced. Sub-headings are 20px bold (3 of the 5 bold-ceiling slots).
- **Bottom meta block in three columns** (PREPARED BY / FOR / DATE), 10px TEXT_FAINT label + 14px TEXT_DARK value, all non-bold. Sits inside the right panel, not in the invariant bottom zone.
- **No personal email, no DRAFT/CONFIDENTIAL** — meta values are role names ("Redesign Working Team"), not personal contact info (`designer-brief § 2`).

**Reach for this when.** Section divider or cover where you want stronger editorial weight than the full-bleed dark cover gives — e.g., the cover needs to ALSO list scope/audience/date without competing with the title. Or any "asymmetric foil" slide where one side asserts and the other side qualifies.

**Patterns to copy.** Full-height left block (`y_px=0, h_px=720`); `anchor="bottom"` on the hero title inside a tall box; meta block as 3-column 10px/14px label/value pairs.
