# Narrative brief: Southeast Asia Entry

## Deck type
Business Case

## Narrative framework
Pyramid (answer first, then the supporting evidence, then the ask). The committee is senior and short on time, and already accepts that Southeast Asia is worth a look; it has not agreed where to start or how much to commit. Runner-up rejected: SCR, because opening with the situation would spend three slides on a market the committee already believes in before reaching the decision.

## Strategic framework used (if any)
A two-bar market screen (market size and readiness) to choose the entry market; gated, staged investment to cap the downside.

## Governing thought (the whole deck)
Meridian should enter Southeast Asia through Vietnam first, then Indonesia, starting with $12M of Phase 1 funding.

## Audience
Meridian's investment committee. They see Southeast Asia as attractive but risky, and lean toward either a large multi-market launch or waiting.

**Audience assumption to break:** "Entering Southeast Asia means a big, all-at-once bet across the region."
**Audience belief to leave with:** "A capped $12M first step in Vietnam, with two stop points, tells us whether to scale before we commit more."
**The single sentence the room should say back:** "Approve $12M for Vietnam now; decide on Indonesia at month 18."
