# Meridian showcase: illustrative data sheet

Every number in the showcase storyline, review page and deck comes from this
sheet, so they tie out. **All of it is invented.** Every slide's source line
reads "Illustrative data, not real". Never attach a real publisher to it.

Meridian is a fictional consumer-goods company deciding how to enter Southeast
Asia.

## 1. Category market size, Southeast Asia ($B)

| 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 | 2030 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | 2.4 | 2.6 | 3.0 | 3.3 | 3.7 | 4.2 |

Growth: 2.1 to 4.2 over six years is **12% a year** (12.2%). The market
**doubles by 2030**. Do not write any other growth rate.

## 2. Five markets against the entry bar

Bar: 2030 market of at least $1.0B **and** readiness of at least 3.5 out of 5.

| Market | 2030 size ($B) | Growth 2024-30 (per year) | Partner availability (1-5) | Distribution (1-5) | Rules for foreign brands (1-5) | Readiness (average) | Clears the bar? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vietnam | 1.2 | 18% | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4.0 | Yes |
| Indonesia | 1.3 | 15% | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3.7 | Yes |
| Thailand | 0.8 | 6% | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4.0 | No (size) |
| Philippines | 0.5 | 11% | 3 | 2 | 3 | 2.7 | No (size, readiness) |
| Malaysia | 0.4 | 5% | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3.3 | No (size, readiness) |

The five 2030 sizes add to 4.2 (section 1).

## 3. Vietnam vs Thailand market size ($M)

| | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 | 2030 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Vietnam | 444 | 524 | 618 | 729 | 861 | 1,016 | 1,200 |
| Thailand | 564 | 598 | 634 | 672 | 712 | 755 | 800 |

Vietnam passes Thailand in **2027** (729 vs 672). By 2030 Vietnam is 1.5 times
Thailand (1,200 vs 800).

Why Vietnam before Indonesia: similar size, but Vietnam grows faster (18% vs
15%) and is more ready (4.0 vs 3.7), so it is the safer first proof.

## 4. The plan in one line

Leading with Vietnam reaches a **$40M revenue run-rate within 18 months** for
**$12M** of Phase 1 investment. Indonesia follows only if Vietnam passes both
gates.

## 5. Phase 1 investment, $12M

| Item | $M |
|---|---|
| Partner onboarding | 2.5 |
| Launch marketing | 4.0 |
| Distribution setup | 3.0 |
| Local team | 1.5 |
| Contingency | 1.0 |
| **Total** | **12.0** |

Spending stays capped at $12M until Gate 2. Phase 2 (Indonesia) is a separate
decision at month 18.

## 6. The 18-month plan and its two gates

| Months | Work |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Choose and sign the Vietnam distribution partner |
| 4-6 | Pilot in Ho Chi Minh City |
| **Month 6: Gate 1** | Pilot sell-through at or above target, or stop |
| 7-12 | National rollout in Vietnam |
| **Month 12: Gate 2** | Contribution margin of at least 15%, or stop |
| 13-18 | Indonesia partner search and entry preparation |
| Month 18 | Phase 2 funding decision |

## 7. The five-step market-entry playbook

1. Pick the partner
2. Prove it in one city
3. Pass the gate
4. Scale nationally
5. Reuse the playbook in the next market

## 8. The partner growth loop

More local partners, then wider distribution, then more sales data, then
better targeting, then a lower cost to enter the next market, then more
partners.
