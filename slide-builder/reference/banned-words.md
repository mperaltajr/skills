# Words that do not go on a slide

Slide Lab content reads like a strong strategy team's work: specific claims,
carried by numbers and named facts. These words sound like a point of view
without stating one. Every step reads this one list: the storyline check, the
brief seal (`seal_brief.py`), the designers, and the quality check.

**The fix is always the same: replace the word with the number or the named
fact it stands for.** "Significant savings" becomes "$4M a year"; "a robust
process" becomes "three checks before release".

One term per line between the markers below. A line ending in `*` matches any
ending (`transform*` catches transformative, transformational). Lines with
`(unless ...)` are flagged for a person to judge, not refused outright.

<!-- judge:start -->
scalable (unless a number says how far)
significant (unless a number follows)

<!-- judge:end -->

## Names

Never name McKinsey, BCG, Bain or "MBB" in anything a user reads or a slide
carries. Describe the method instead ("growth-share matrix", "one claim per
slide").
