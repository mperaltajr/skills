DRAFT

Interview Insights Synthesis — Themes from Research

Qualitative signal distilled from leader interviews across three organisational levels
N = 24 interviews
3 levels: Senior Leadership · Middle Management · Frontline
6-week field programme · May 2026
Clarity & Direction
Mentioned by 20 of 24 leaders
“We keep changing direction. People don’t know what success looks like six months out, let alone three years.”
— VP, Strategy & Planning
“My team asks me every week: what is the actual priority? I don’t always have a clean answer, and that’s the problem.”
— Senior Manager, Operations
Synthesized Insight
Strategic ambiguity is fracturing execution — leaders at every level cite direction uncertainty as the primary performance drag.
Capability Gaps
Mentioned by 17 of 24 leaders
“We have talent, but not the skills for what we’re being asked to do now. The reskilling pipeline is too slow.”
— Director, People & Change
“Data literacy is non-negotiable for my team’s mandate. Right now, maybe two people in ten have it at the level we need.”
— Head of Analytics, Finance
Synthesized Insight
The skill base lags the strategic ask by 18–24 months; leaders view the reskilling pace as the single largest execution risk.
Trust & Psychological Safety
Mentioned by 15 of 24 leaders
“People have stopped raising problems early because the last few times, the messenger got shot. That’s a culture issue, not a process issue.”
— Associate Director, Risk
“There’s a performance theatre dynamic — everyone looks busy, but no one is sure they’re working on the right things.”
— Programme Manager, Delivery
Synthesized Insight
Suppressed dissent is masking early-warning signals; psychological safety deficits are compounding both direction and capability risks.
Consistent signal
Across all three themes: leaders feel the organisation is operating at pace without a shared map — direction, capability, and candour must be addressed together, not sequentially.
