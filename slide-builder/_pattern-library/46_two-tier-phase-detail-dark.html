What happens in this phase
1
Template intake — drop your client .pptx; fonts, colors and layouts read automatically.
2
Opening question — "what are you trying to communicate?" starts the structured conversation.
3
Audience + assumed knowledge — who sees it and what they already know going in.
4
Infer + confirm — page type, mode and governing thought reflected back, then confirmed.
5
Deepen structure — 2-4 supporting buckets, evidence and risks locked in.
→ Structured brief produced (complete handoff document)
What happens in this phase
6
Reads brief + design rules — applies MBB slide conventions and page-type rules to your content.
7
Generates 3-4 visual mockup variations — each a structurally distinct layout bet, with trade-offs explained.
8
You select + refine — react, combine options or request changes; nothing locks until you approve.
→ Approved mockup produced (visual structure + content confirmed)
What happens in this phase
9
Builds slide in PPTX XML — uses your exact client template: fonts, colors, placeholder positions and dimensions.
10
QA'd as PDF before delivery — checked for overlaps, font errors and leftover placeholder text.
11
Delivered to your folder — clickable file link, opens in PowerPoint, ready to present.
→ Finished .pptx on your client template
