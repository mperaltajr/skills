90-day plan — three phases, one runway from foundation to scale.
Days 1–30  ·  “Roll down the runway”
Days 31–60  ·  “Liftoff”
Days 61–90  ·  “Initial climb”
Phase
Objective
Key actions
Output · proof of progress
Days 1–30
“Access & foundation”
Establish trusted access at senior levels and align on business priorities.
Align on priority domains; conduct executive listening sessions led by account leadership.
Focus discussions on outcome gaps, value leakage, and partner blind spots.
Establish a monthly executive touch-point cadence anchored on the ops-to-value connection.
Shared view of executive priorities & pain points
1–2 senior sponsors aligned to approach
Days 31–60
“Credibility & proof”
Demonstrate value and shift perception from delivery vendor to trusted advisor.
Deliver targeted, domain-specific insights via lightweight POVs focused on problem, impact, implication.
Facilitate decision-focused working sessions with leaders — not presentations.
Embed insights directly into active programs and live discussions.
Leaders begin pulling us into discussions
Evidence of improved decisions and clarity on value drivers
Days 61–90
“Scale & positioning”
Institutionalize the role in the operating rhythm and decision-making cycle.
Launch quarterly operational value reviews — jointly owned with the client.
Position as connector across operations, transformation, and value.
Embed capability into account governance and investment-prioritization cycles.
Consistently present in decision-making forums
Role embedded in operating model — expected, not optional
