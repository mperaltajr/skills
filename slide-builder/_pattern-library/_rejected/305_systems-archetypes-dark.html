Causal Loop Diagram
R Loop — growth reinforces itself
B Loop — constraint slows growth
Archetype: Limits to Growth
Growth stalls when
constraints go unaddressed
A reinforcing engine drives progress — until a hidden
limiting condition applies the brakes.
1
Reinforcing loop (R): greater effort produces better performance, which justifies further effort — a self-amplifying cycle.
2
Balancing loop (B): as performance grows it strains the limiting factor (capacity, skill, resource), which erodes the effect of future effort.
3
Trap: leaders push harder on the R loop. The system appears to plateau for no apparent reason; the real fix is removing the constraint.
Management Leverage
Identify and relieve the limiting factor early — before performance plateaus. Pushing harder on the growth action after the constraint binds yields diminishing returns and organizational fatigue.
