## STANDING QC CHECKLIST — every pattern must satisfy all six

1. **Visual hierarchy clear** — title, body, footnote weights distinguishable
2. **Single focal point** — eye lands in one place
3. **Margins consistent** — same gutters and edges throughout
4. **Whitespace intentional** — not leftover, not cramped
5. **Elements align to a visible grid** — parallel things share x-coords + baselines
6. **Page balanced** — not top-heavy or one-sided
