How the Flywheel Drives Compounding Growth

Each stage reinforces the next — momentum builds until the cycle becomes self-sustaining

How the loop works

A flywheel is a self-reinforcing system where each stage feeds the next. The more the wheel spins, the harder it is to stop — momentum compounds over time and the advantages become structural.

  • Customer — better experience raises satisfaction and referrals
  • Demand — higher volume creates scale advantages
  • Efficiency — scale reduces marginal cost per transaction
  • Investment — freed-up capital is reinvested into capability
  • Capability — broader services raise the customer experience bar
