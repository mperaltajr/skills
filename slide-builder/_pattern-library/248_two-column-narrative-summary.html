Data Architecture: Strategic Readout

Executive summary of diagnostic findings, implications, and recommended path forward

Context

The organization has operated under a fragmented data architecture for 5+ years, resulting in 23 disparate systems and an estimated $8M in annual reconciliation costs.

Findings

Our diagnostic reveals three root causes: (1) absence of a master data standard, (2) siloed ownership across business units, and (3) underinvestment in integration middleware.

Implications

Without intervention, the cost trajectory reaches $14M by 2028 and data quality issues will constrain the planned AI initiatives scheduled for 2027.

So What?
Immediate structural intervention is required — the current architecture is a strategic liability that will actively block AI transformation goals.
Recommended Next Steps

  2. 2
    Establish cross-BU data governance council
  3. 3
    Procure integration platform (RFP by July)
