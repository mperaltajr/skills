What changed in four weeks of pilot.
Same teams, same deck volume. Different discipline — and the workflow followed.
Before · Week 0
Disconnected deck workflow
  • Slide deck assembled bottom-up from workstream findings
  • Argument lost as bullets compound across review cycles
  • Three rounds of partner edits per deck typical
  • 11 days median from kickoff to final
Pilot
Weeks 1–4
After · Week 4
Structured argument-first workflow
  • Governing thought drives the deck structure
  • Each slide carries one argument; reviews sharpen, not pile on
  • One round of partner edits
  • 5 days median from kickoff to final
The change isn't a tool — it's a discipline. The tool just enforces it.
