2. Pick brand colors

Click a swatch to assign it. Each row picks one color from the template's palette. The auto-best-guess is pre-selected (purple ring). Auto-pick has historically inverted on most client templates — if the preview above looked wrong, this is where you correct it.
Primary brand color
The dominant brand color. Used for title bars, headers, dark-canvas fills, anything that should read as "this is a <client> deck." For most clients this is the color in their logo and on their website headers (e.g. FedEx purple, Accenture purple, IBM blue).
Accent / highlight color
The "look at this" color. Used sparingly — one accent moment per slide (a single hero KPI, a highlight stripe, a recommended row). Never for body text or background. Typically the brand's secondary color (e.g. FedEx orange, Accenture violet/magenta). Should contrast strongly with primary.
Cover-slide background color
The fill behind the cover slide only. Drives the dramatic full-bleed background on slide 1 (the deck title page). Most often the same as primary, but some brands prefer a lighter or darker variant for the cover. Default to primary unless you have a specific cover style in mind.

4. Strip baked-in master backgrounds?

Many client templates ship with photographic decoration baked into the slide master (stadium photos, building shots, brand watermarks). Those bleed through behind every slide and break clean layouts. Default: strip. Untick only if the decoration is intentional brand chrome.
