I spent hours diagnosing whether FedEx's gradient bands were master-shape vs master-background-fill, whether `showMasterSp="0"` was suppressing them, etc. **None of that mattered.** `FDX Template.pptx` has **plain white master backgrounds and zero decorative chrome shapes anywhere.** The bands Mario sees in finished decks come from `OTC Opportunity.pptx`, which has chrome on **layouts** (Rectangle 4/5/6 on the body layout) AND on inline slide content.

The architecture I built for "make master chrome flow through" was solving a problem `FDX Template.pptx` doesn't have. When Mario builds against FDX Template, there is no chrome to inherit because FDX never had any.
